## Health score formula

```
Health Score = 100 - (CRITICAL*10 + HIGH*5 + MEDIUM*2 + LOW*1) / Total_Pages
```

Example: 50 pages, 2 critical / 3 high / 5 medium / 10 low → deductions 55 → score 98.9%.

Target: >90% is healthy.
