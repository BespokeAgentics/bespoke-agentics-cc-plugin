# Product brief — Digital gift cards

## Goal

Let shoppers buy digital gift cards and send them by email. Recipients redeem them at checkout.

## Users

- **Purchaser** — signed in or checking out as a guest.
- **Recipient** — may not have an account.
- **Support agent** — handles cancellations and refunds.

## Requirements

1. The purchaser chooses an amount between $10 and $500, in whole dollars.
2. The purchaser enters the recipient's email address and an optional message (up to 200 characters).
3. Delivery can be immediate or scheduled for a date up to 90 days ahead.
4. The recipient receives a 16-character code. Codes never expire.
5. At checkout, a gift card can pay for all or part of an order. Any remaining balance stays on the card.
6. Support can cancel an unredeemed gift card and refund the purchaser.

## Success metric

Gift-card revenue reaches 3% of total revenue within two quarters.

## Out of scope

Physical cards. Currencies other than USD.
